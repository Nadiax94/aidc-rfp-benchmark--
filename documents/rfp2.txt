SECTION A – INTRODUCTION 
 
Purpose 
This Request for Proposal (RFP) is intended to identify interested suppliers and allow them the 
opportunity to competitively submit a Proposal to this RFP, as a Proponent.  
 
Olds College of Agriculture and Technology (Olds College) is seeking to enhance its existing learning 
management system (LMS) capabilities; either through the enhancement of third-party support for its 
existing Moodle LMS, or via a new LMS system, to support an improved student learning experience, 
increased instructor support, and the ability to expand institutional access to distance learning 
markets.  
Values Statement 
At Olds College we value:  
●​ Empowerment of learners 
●​ Rural community development 
●​ Teamwork 
●​ Continuous improvement in all College operations 
Mission Statement  
Alberta's agriculture community has the talent, knowledge and thought leadership to lead globally. This 
result will be produced in a manner that demonstrates stewardship and sustainability. 
 
Social Purpose 
The Social Purpose of Olds College is “Transforming agriculture for a better world”. 
Everything we do at Olds College is done to advance all aspects of the agriculture industry.  By 
transforming the agriculture industry, we will make the world a better place.  All of our programs and 
actions align to our social purpose.  It is the why behind everything we do. 
Strategic Plan - Strategic Drivers 
In 2018, Olds College launched a strategic plan: Growing 2025. The plan was developed and built on the 
college’s social purpose to “Transform agriculture for a better world,” and reaffirms that everything we 
do at Olds College is done to advance the agriculture industry, both here at home and throughout the 
globe. 
For more information about Growing 2025: the Olds College Strategic Plan 
 
About Olds College  
"For more than 100 years Olds College has delivered hands-on training, innovative learning programs, 
and graduates who are in-demand and have the skills and tools needed to succeed. Olds College 
continues to embrace this model of practical, hands-on technical education where students learn by 
doing. Virtually every program has some element of practical experience, which helps prepare our 
students for successful careers in the real world. 
 
Today, Olds College is known for high-tech, hands-on agriculture education and innovative applied 
research that lays the foundation for solving real-world problems in farming, food and land. Olds College

General Requirements 
 
3.1​ Background Information 
The purpose and intent of this RFP is to solicit proposals from qualified vendors to enhance the 
College’s existing learning management system (LMS) capabilities; either through the enhancement of 
third-party support for its existing Moodle LMS, or via a new LMS system.  
 
The College is planning to commence either the enhancement of its existing LMS, or begin the process 
of implementing a new LMS, by the 1st of February, 2027. 
 
Olds College is home to over 3400 students across 40+ programs that occur throughout the year.  
 
The College currently utilizes Moodle LMS, an open-source learning management system providing 
educators and institutions with a customizable platform to create online courses, track student progress, 
and manage collaborative learning environments.  
RFP 197/2027 – Learning Management System (LMS) Platform & Support                 Page | 7    
 
Core Features Include 
●​ Course Creation: Build structured learning paths using interactive tools, text pages, multimedia, 
and lesson modules. 
●​ Assessments & Grading: Administer graded and ungraded quizzes, track assignments, and utilize 
integrated grade books with custom rubrics. 
●​ Collaboration & Engagement: Foster collaboration, knowledge sharing, and engagement through 
interactive tools that facilitate multiple means of student engagement and expression, such 
as discussion forms, wikis, workshops, and gamification elements  
●​ Accessibility & Privacy: Meet WCAG standards with built-in accessibility checks, secure data 
controls, and compliance tools. 
The College leverages a third-party firm to support the current open-source LMS; along with a small 
team of in-house LMS professionals to provide ongoing support to instructors and students.  
Note, the College utilizes Ellucian Banner as its Student Information System (SIS). Any proposed LMS 
enhancement or new LMS option would need to integrate with this SIS.
